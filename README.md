## How I work

1. **Requirements first.** Real pain points, roles and workflows are mapped before anything is designed.
2. **Schema first.** The data model is designed and reviewed before any screen: strict types, foreign keys, indexes.
3. **Secure by default.** Prepared statements, server-side role checks, CSRF protection, rate-limited logins, full audit trail.
4. **Fast with real data.** Batched reads and writes, indexed lookups, no queries inside loops.
5. **Delivered in phases.** Clients see working software early and often, then get training and support after launch.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who is Mohammad Rameez Imdad?

I am a software developer from Lahore, Pakistan and the founder of Rameez Scripts. I hold a BS in Computer Science from Minhaj University Lahore and have delivered 400+ projects since 2022 for clients in 50+ countries.

### What does Rameez Scripts build?

Custom ERP systems, CRM systems, POS and inventory software, HR and payroll systems, and school, hospital and hotel management systems, plus N8N workflows, AI automation, MCP servers and WhatsApp automation. Most systems are built in Google Apps Script with Google Sheets or in PHP 8 with MySQL.

### What is an MCP server, and can you build one for my business?

An MCP (Model Context Protocol) server lets an AI assistant such as Claude use your own business system: it can read your data and run tasks through tools you approve. Yes, I build custom MCP servers for websites, databases and APIs.

### Can I buy a ready-made system with source code?

Yes. [rameezscripts.com](https://rameezscripts.com) lists 380+ ready-made projects with full source code, in Google Apps Script and in PHP and MySQL.

### Google Apps Script or PHP and MySQL: which one fits my business?

Google Apps Script with Google Sheets suits a small team that wants a web app with no server to manage. PHP and MySQL suits larger data, many users working at the same time and public-facing websites. I recommend one after the requirements call.
